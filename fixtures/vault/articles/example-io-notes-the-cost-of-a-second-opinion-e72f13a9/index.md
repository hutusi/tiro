Every team I have worked on treats a second opinion as free. It is not: the
consultation costs an hour, and the decision it postpones costs whatever the
delay costs, which is almost never written down anywhere.

## Three tests

The first test is whether the answer could change what you do. If both replies
lead to the same next step, you are not gathering evidence, you are gathering
company.

1.  Could the answer change the action?
2.  What does the delay cost per day?
3.  Is "proceed" the honest default?

The second test is the one people skip. A week of waiting is not free just
because nobody invoices for it.

> The expensive part of a decision is rarely the decision.

The third test is a habit rather than a calculation. Teams that default to
asking will always find one more person to ask.
