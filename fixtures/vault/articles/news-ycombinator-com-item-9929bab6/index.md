I clip a lot of articles and almost never open them again. What does your system look like, and what made it stick?

> **margin_notes** · [2026-10-01](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=1000000001)
>
> I stopped saving whole pages. I write two sentences on why it mattered, and those two sentences are what I reread.

> > **plaintext_fan** · [2026-10-01](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=1000000002)
> >
> > Same here. The summary is the index; the page is the appendix.

> > > **margin_notes** · [2026-10-02](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=1000000003)
> > >
> > > And an appendix you can search is still worth keeping, so I save the page anyway.

> **weekly_review** · [2026-10-02](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=1000000004)
>
> A fixed hour every Friday. Anything I have not reread in a month gets deleted.
>
> It sounds harsh, but the pile finally stopped growing.
